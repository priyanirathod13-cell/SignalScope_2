## 5. Key Forensic Observations

1. **Massive Reduction in False Alarms**: The biggest practical problem in V1 was misclassifying authentic camera photos as AI (31.4% false positive rate). V2 cut this rate down to 14.1%, while lifting Real photo F1 from 0.7146 to 0.8817.
2. **Latent Diffusion Detectability**: Models generated by Stable Diffusion (v1.4 and v1.5) are detected with exceptionally high fidelity (96.2% on SD 1.4, 84.0% on SD 1.5).
3. **Pixel-Space Diffusion Challenge**: Ablated Diffusion Model (`adm`) remains the most subtle generative architecture (62.07% accuracy). Unlike latent diffusion models which leave distinct high-frequency autoencoder artifacts, guided pixel-space diffusion produces fewer latents-to-pixel reconstruction artifacts.
4. **Generalisation vs. Memorisation**: The steady improvement across both CIFAKE (+93.8%) and GenImage (+73.8%) on held-out test splits confirms true feature generalization rather than rote memorization.
